Quizinho Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Quizinho is a football party game made by Little Fort Studios Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17149145). This policy explains what the app does with information when you play. In short: there are no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and we don't collect anything that identifies you.

What stays on your phone

Your settings, team names, your last match setup, a match in progress and your solo personal bests are saved on your own device only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Your microphone and spoken answers

If you choose to say your answers out loud, Quizinho asks for microphone and speech recognition permission. Your voice is turned into text so the game can check your answer.

  • On iPhone, speech is recognised on your device by Apple's speech recognition.
  • On Android phones and in web browsers, speech is recognised by your device's or browser's own speech service (for example Google's), under that provider's privacy policy.

We never record or store your voice. You can always type answers instead, and you can switch voice answers off in Settings.

Playing on your own phones (game rooms)

If the host chooses "Play on your own phones", the game creates a room with a four-letter code so other phones can join. While the game is on, the room holds the team names and player names you typed, which phone picked which name (a random code, not your device's identity), buzzes and answers, and the scores.

Rooms are stored in Google Firebase Realtime Database, hosted in the EU (Belgium). Anyone with the room code can see the room while it exists, so don't put anything private in team or player names. Rooms are deleted automatically within about a day.

Helping the voice recognition get better

When a spoken answer isn't recognised as any player, the app saves the words the speech recogniser heard (for example "deli for you"), the right answers to that question, the footballer picked instead if there was one, and the type of device (iPhone, Android or web). Nothing else is saved — no names, no device identifiers, no audio, nothing that could identify you. We use these to teach the game how real players' names get misheard, so the right answer counts next time.

Purchases

If you buy the full game, the payment is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics, and no selling or sharing of data.

Children

Quizinho is a general-audience party game and isn't aimed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what we hold about you and ask us to delete it. Because Quizinho doesn't collect information that identifies you, we usually can't link any data to a particular person — but get in touch and we'll help however we can.
